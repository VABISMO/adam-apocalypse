On the discard. "Why Genesis and not another combination?" — structural answer: the discard is positional (S ⊆ O). Genesis is chosen because its list of simples coincides with the hinge of the precessional ages: Aquarius (צ, incoming era) and Aries (טלה, preceding era).

On the 19. The 19 unites Meton, Saros, and the basmala (§8): it is the number of lunar synchrony. 144 (§14) is the number of the completeness of 12 applied to the moon (144 months of Meton) and to the circle (144 000 = 360×400). 72 is the precessional degree (§3) and the Name of 72 letters.
