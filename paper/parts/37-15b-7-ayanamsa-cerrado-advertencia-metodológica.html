15b.7 Ayanamsa — CLOSED (methodological warning)

§11 dates the eras with Lahiri (24.18°). Compared 4 ayanamsas (~2024): Lahiri 24.18°, Krishnamurti 23.93°, Fagan-Bradley 25.06°, Raman 22.40° (calc_ayanamsa.mjs). The entry date of each era shifts Δt = Δayanamsa / precession: the entry into Aquarius oscillates between 2378 CE (Fagan-Bradley) and 2568 CE (Raman) — a dispersion of 190 years (max Δayanamsa 2.66° / 0.01397°/year). Consequence: the "Age of Aquarius" as a single date is not an empirical fact but a convention (it depends on the chosen sidereal zero). This reinforces the design of the Reader of the Sky: its discard is positional on tropical signs (occupation of the 7 classical bodies, §10), independent of the ayanamsa — hence robust to this convention. Invariants (independent of the ayanamsa): great year 25 771 y, era 2147.5 y. Outstanding matter closed ✓ (as a warning, not as a datum).
