15b.5 37×73 structure of the 7 words of Genesis 1:1 (Jenkins)

Facts: 2701 = 37×73 = T₇₃; 28 letters = T₇; 7×28 = 196 = 14². The strong claim —that the partition of the 28 letters into these 7 words is biased toward multiples of 37— is tested with a rigorous null: the multiset of the 28 letters and the word lengths (6,3,5,2,5,3,4) are preserved, the letters are permuted and regrouped (100 000×, deterministic PRNG). Statistics: (A) 2 of the 7 word-sums are multiples of 37 (407=11×37, 296=8×37 — the last two: ואת, הארץ); under the null, P(≥2/7) = 8.2×10⁻³. (B) 23 of the 127 non-empty subsets are multiples of 37 (chance ~3.4); under the null, P(≥23) = 3.1×10⁻⁴ (only 31/100 000 permutations equal or exceed). Verdict: the 37×73 structure of the 7 words is empirically real beyond the trivial total fact 2701=37×73. The partition is biased toward 37; the composition of the first verse is not arbitrary with respect to that factor.
