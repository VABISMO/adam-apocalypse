9.3 Genesis-days / eclipses correlation (empirical test)

With the 7 classical bodies the SY assigns to the 7 doubles (Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn — Uranus, Neptune, Pluto are not in the SY and no Hebrew letter is assigned to them, so the apparatus excludes them entirely), Genesis 1:1 is legible only 2 days in 2024–2030 (2/2557 = 0.078 %, ~0.29 days/year — genuinely rare: it requires {י,ה,ל,ו,צ} = Virgo+Aries+Libra+Taurus+Aquarius, five simple letters whose five signs must be all simultaneously occupied by the seven bodies). The 2 legible days (2028-03-12, 2029-04-08) were contrasted with the 34 eclipses of the same period (astronomy-engine: global solar + lunar eclipses). Genesis days with an eclipse within ±10 days: 0/2 (0 %). Random expectation (eclipses/year × 21-day window / 365): 28.0 %. Both Genesis-legible days avoid eclipse windows — with n=2 this is suggestive rather than significant, but consistent in direction: the engine of Genesis legibility is the position of the 7 planets across 5 specific signs, not the Sun–Moon–node geometry that produces eclipses. (The earlier 10-body figure of 293 legible days (11.5 %) depended on Neptune and Pluto anchoring Aquarius and Aries; those two modern planets carry no SY letter and are removed — the rare 7-body legibility above is the apparatus's real result.)
