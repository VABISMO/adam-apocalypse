9.3 Genesis 1:1 legibility (empirical)

With the 7 classical bodies the SY assigns to the 7 doubles (Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn — Uranus, Neptune, Pluto are not in the SY and no Hebrew letter is assigned to them, so the apparatus excludes them entirely), Genesis 1:1 is legible only 2 days in 2024–2030 (2/2557 = 0.078 %, ~0.29 days/year — genuinely rare: it requires {י,ה,ל,ו,צ} = Virgo+Aries+Libra+Taurus+Aquarius, five simple letters whose five signs must be all simultaneously occupied by the seven bodies). The engine of Genesis legibility is the position of the 7 planets across 5 specific signs.
