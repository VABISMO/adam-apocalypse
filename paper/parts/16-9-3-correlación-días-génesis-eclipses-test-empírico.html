9.3 Genesis-days / eclipses correlation (empirical test)

The 293 Genesis-legible days (2024–2030) were contrasted with the 34 eclipses of the same period (astronomy-engine: global solar + lunar eclipses). Genesis days with an eclipse within ±10 days: 7.5 % (22/293). Random expectation (eclipses/year × 21-day window / 365): 28.0 %. Genesis days avoid eclipse windows; the engine is the position of the slow planets, not the Sun–Moon–node geometry.
