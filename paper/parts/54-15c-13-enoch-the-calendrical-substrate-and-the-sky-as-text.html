15c.13 Enoch: the calendrical substrate and the sky as text

The Revelation–Raziel–SY sky-as-text lineage traced through §15c has an older layer: Enoch. The Enochic corpus (1 Enoch / Ethiopic; 2 Enoch / Slavonic; 3 Enoch / Hebrew) supplies the calendrical-astronomical substrate on which the later letter↔star reading of the SY and Raziel is built, and it formulates the “sky as a written record” image independently and earlier than John. The coincidences are structural and verifiable; the layering is genealogical, not a borrowing claim.

Enoch as a 365-cipher. Enoch’s lifespan is 365 years (Genesis 5:23-24), the civil year — the same 365 = 73×5 of §9.4. The identification Enoch = the solar year is an attested tradition (Philo, De posteritate Caini; 2 Enoch 1:2 “all the days of Enoch’s life were 365”). The paper’s 365-pentad structure and its “Enoch” reference share a root: the number 365 attaches to Enoch the person before it attaches to any calendar.

The 364-day year — the oldest Jewish scientific text. The Book of the Luminaries (1 Enoch 72–82) sets a 364-day year = 52 weeks = 4×91 (72:32; 74:12; 82:6; “364 stations” 75:2), broader than Qumran: attested at Masada (terminus ante quem 73 CE) and in Jubilees, paleographically pre-Maccabean (4Q208, radiocarbon ~186 BCE). This is the calendrical bedrock beneath the SY’s 360/7/12 scheme (§5, §7): a 364-day, twelve-gated, weekly-partitioned heaven.

The twelve gates of the sun. 1 Enoch 72 places the sun behind twelve gates — six east, six west — one 30-day gate per month, the annual circuit through twelve portals. A twelve-fold solar partition of the year, structurally cognate to the twelve simple letters / twelve signs (§6), expressed as gates rather than as letters.

360 explicit, and stars that transgress. 1 Enoch 82:11 names the 360 days with their “heads over thousands who divide the days” (364 minus the four epagomenal); the paper’s 360 (§3, §9.6) is explicit here. And 1 Enoch 80:6-7 — “many chiefs of the stars shall transgress the order” — gives the eschatological disorder: at the end, the luminaries break their appointed courses. This is the same end-times cosmic disturbance as Revelation’s falling stars and the unsealed sixth seal (§15c.4-5): stars that transgress = the sky itself turning against its own order.

The sky as a written record — the heavenly tablets. 1 Enoch is the earliest full formulation of the image the paper traces through John, Raziel, and the Apocalypse of Adam: the sky as a book. The “heavenly tablets” record the deeds and destinies of all mankind (81:1-2), periodize history into ten weeks (93:2), and are read, copied, and invoked (103:2-3; 106-107; 108:3,7,10). John’s sealed scroll (§15c.1), Raziel’s “calculate to see the generations” (§15c.8), and Adam’s “every birth of their ruler is a word” (§15c.1) are later faces of this Enochic tablet — the sky as a legible, generational record. Enoch formulates it first.

3 Enoch: creation by letters, including the luminaries. 3 Enoch (Hebrew Enoch, 5th-6th c. CE) makes the letter-creation explicit and astral: God creates by letters, and among the created letters are “the planets and the constellations” (3 Enoch 13:1) — a direct parallel to the SY’s “He created with twenty-two letters” extended to the heavenly bodies. Metatron-Enoch, the “lesser YHWH” and scribe of righteousness with seventy names, is the angelic-scribal figure in which the Enochic and the Yetziratic traditions converge.

Layering, not borrowing. 1 Enoch 72–82 is calendrical — gates of the sun, a 364-day year, numbered portals (Neugebauer 1981: no constellations, zodiac, or planets; notation by numbered gate, not by letter). The letter↔star reading is the Yetziratic-Raziel layer (§15c.8, §15c.10) built on the Enochic substrate. Enoch supplies the calendar and the sky-as-text image; the SY and Raziel supply the alphabet that reads it. The design stands in layers — and Enoch is the oldest extant layer of the same construction.
