nsCheckTable — what is demonstrated beyond all doubt, and what is not

A single, consolidated status table. Demonstrated = reproducible by calculation and/or survives its null at conventional significance. Tested, not demonstrated = a claim the project tested and the data does not support — reported as a negative, so the article does not over-state. Open = not closeable by elementary calculation and not asserted. Every row points to its script and data file. This table supersedes prose for the purpose of "what is actually proven".
