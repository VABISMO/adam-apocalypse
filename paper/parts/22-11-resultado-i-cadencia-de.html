11. Result I — Cadence of יהוה

S(יהוה) = {י,ה,ו} = Virgo + Aries + Taurus. The Moon traverses the 12 signs every 27.3 days, so those three signs are occupied most of the year, with brief voids → ~monthly cadence. יהוה does not mark eras: it marks the month. Its three signs are filled by fast planets.

Relation to the tetramorph (§6): the two Heh of יהוה are Aries (טלה, ram) and the Vav is Taurus (שור, ox); the Yod is Virgo (בתולה). The Name is thus composed of ram + ox + virgin.
