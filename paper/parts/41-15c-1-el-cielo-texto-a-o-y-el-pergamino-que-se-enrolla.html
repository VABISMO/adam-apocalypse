15c.1 The sky-text: Α, Ω, and the scroll that rolls up

Rev 6:14: "the sky vanished like a scroll that is being rolled up, and every mountain and island was removed from its place", preceded by "the stars of the sky fell to the earth as the fig tree sheds its winter fruit". The sky is a book (βιβλίον); the stars are the letters; rolling up the scroll = the zodiac band (the 12 simples) closing and the letters coming loose from their seats. "I am the Alpha and the Omega, the first and the last" (Rev 1:8; 21:6; 22:13) = the bounds of the alphabet = the totality of the letters = the cosmos as text. In the SY this is literal: "with 22 letters He formed the whole of His creation." The Lamb who takes the sealed scroll and opens it (Rev 5) is the one who can read the sky as a book = the Lector Caeli.

The Sethian parallel — the Apocalypse of Adam. A contemporary gnostic text, the Apocalypse of Adam (Nag Hammadi V,5), frames the same idea from outside the Johannine line: the thirteenth kingdom says of the illuminator that “every birth of their ruler is a word. And this word received a mandate there. He received glory and power. And thus he came to the water, in order that the desire of those powers might be satisfied.” The ruler's birth is a word — the prophet, the era, the celestial event is a readable letter-string. This is the Sethian register the present work adopts as its epigraph: the sky writes the generations, and the one who can read the word is the one who comes to the water. The convergence is structural, not genealogical — Revelation (the sealed scroll), Raziel (calculate to see the generations, §15c.8), and the Apocalypse of Adam (the ruler is a word) are three formulations of one idea: the sky is text, and the astronomer is its reader.
