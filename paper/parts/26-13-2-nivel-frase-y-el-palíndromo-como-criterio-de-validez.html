13.2 Phrase level and the palindrome as a validity criterion

Two methodological objections require going beyond the word level: (i) the real units of the text are phrases, verses, and large numbers, not isolated words; (ii) the hypothesis that the mirror-palindrome is the method to distinguish which readings of the sky are valid and which are not (discarding thousands of combinations), not a checksum.

(i) Phrase level. Computed over the 1533 verses of Genesis (calc_phrase.mjs), broken down by digits of the verse gematria:

levelunitmirror-palindrome
word6045 roots (lexicon)47.9 %
phraseverses of Genesis (n=1533)39.3 %
large phraseverses with gematria ≥ 1000 (n=1503)39.6 %

The phrase is more selective than the word (39.3 % vs 47.9 %): the objection is correct in direction. But the rarefaction stops there —long verses (gematria ≥ 1000, already 4 digits) do not palindrome less than the total (39.6 % vs 39.3 %)—, and the rate remains indistinguishable from the Markov null (39.2 %, §13). The magnitude of the number, by itself, does not make the mirror rare.

(ii) The palindrome as a selector of readings. Direct test: the "phrase of the sky" at a date = the simple letters of the 10 bodies, ordered; its gematria + mirror. Comparing Genesis-legible (valid) vs non-legible (invalid) days over 2024–2030:

day classmirror-palindrome
Genesis-legible (n=293)24.9 %
Genesis-non-legible (n=2264)41.1 %

If the palindrome were the validity criterion, the legible ones would palindrome more. They palindrome far less (24.9 % < 41.1 %). The palindrome does not select the valid readings. What does select them —and discards 88.5 % of dates (§10)— is the positional rule S ⊆ O. The mirror and set membership are independent criteria; the second operates, the first does not separate.

Synthesis. The mirror-palindrome is real in specific cases (2701→3773, Egel→404) and the phrase is more selective than the word (39 % vs 48 %), but it does not suffice to be either a checksum or a criterion of reading validity: it does not distinguish from a Markov null, it does not rarefy with magnitude, and it does not separate legible from invalid days. The operational discard of the Reader of the Sky is positional (S ⊆ O), not arithmetic. What is attested and, for the 72 verses, demonstrated, is the intentional letter engineering as a practice (§6.3) —a historical fact, distinct from the statistical selectivity of the mirror.
