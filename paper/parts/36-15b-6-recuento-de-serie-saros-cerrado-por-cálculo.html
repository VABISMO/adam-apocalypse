15b.6 Saros series count by calculation

The saros series count, demonstrated by calculation (calc_saros_series.mjs): all solar eclipses (incl. partials) are detected in [−500, 4500] CE —at each new moon (SearchMoonPhase lon=0) the geocentric lunar ecliptic latitude |β| is measured; eclipse if |β|<1.6° (threshold calibrated to 2.39/year ≈ 2.38 of NASA; ~12 780 eclipses detected)— and chained by the saros period (223 synodics = 6585.32 d): two eclipses of the same series are separated by ~6585.32 d. Of the resulting chains, 152 complete series (border ends |β|>1.3° and length ≥ 50) give lengths 54–87 eclipses, mean 74.4, median 72, with 64 % in the 69–75 peak. It coincides with the 70–86 range / central value ~72–73 of the literature. The "73" is a statistical count (mean/median of members per series), not a period or a factor of eclipse.
