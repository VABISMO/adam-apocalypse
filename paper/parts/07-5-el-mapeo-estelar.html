Doubles↔planet: Saturn=ב, Jupiter=ג, Mars=ד, Sun=כ, Venus=פ, Mercury=ר, Moon=ת.

Mothers↔circumpolar constellation: א→Draco (268°), מ→Ursa Minor (89°), ש→Cassiopeia (38°) — ecliptic longitude of each constellation's centre, J2000. The mothers are assigned to circumpolar constellations off the zodiacal grid, so they are exempt from the precessional sign-rotation of the 12 simples (a design decision: they do not participate in the tropical sectors). The north celestial pole itself precesses on a 25 771-year circuit around the ecliptic pole; "fixed" marks their exemption from the sign grid, not an absence of motion.

Tropical vs. IAU note. The 12 simples are mapped to tropical sectors of 30° (section 4), not to IAU constellations. It is a design decision necessary for the one-to-one letter↔sign grid; applying IAU boundaries (13, unequal) would destroy the premise. The mothers, by contrast, are indeed placed against their real constellations.
