15c.9 The two registers of the Name: the eternal and the temporal

The reading rule opens a theological contrast that the apparatus makes measurable. Of the 22 letters, 3 are mothers (aleph, mem, shin — the primordial elements, fixed, circumpolar) and 7 are doubles (bet, gimel, dalet, kaf, pe, resh, tav — the 7 planets, always available); these 10 do not depend on the zodiac. The remaining 12 are simples — the 12 zodiacal signs, occupied or empty as the planets turn. A word built only from mothers and doubles is therefore always readable: it transcends the sky. A word built from simples is gated: it is readable only when its signs are occupied — i.e., in time.

The always-readable tier holds the theological anchors — and, among them, precisely these:

Two arithmetical facts close the circle:

  • Truth (אמת) = 441 = 21², and 21 = C(7,2) = seals + trumpets + bowls (§15c.3). The rabbinic dictum “the seal of the Holy One, blessed be He, is Truth (אמת)” is the same 21 that structures the sealed scroll of Revelation — the seal, squared.
  • Sabbath (שבת) = 702 = 27 × 26: the alphabet complete with its finals (27) multiplied by יהוה (26). Rest is the whole language × the Name.

And the contrast that fixes the architecture: יהוה is built entirely of simples — yod (Virgo), he (Aries), vav (Taurus) — three temporal letters, none of the eternal ones. The Name is therefore never always-readable: it is bound to the turning sky, legible only in its windows (the ~monthly cadence of §11, “the cadence of יהוה”).

The sealed scroll of Revelation is the zodiac, and the names divide into two registers exactly as the scroll does:

  • the eternal register (mothers + doubles): create, father, mother, sabbath, truth (and fire, daughter, head, heir…) — readable always, needing no opening of the scroll;
  • the temporal register (the 12 simples, the zodiac that turns): יהוה — the Name of “who was and is and is to come” (Rev 1:4), readable only when the sky computes it, only when the scroll is opened.

Truth is the seal (441 = 21²); the Name is what the seal guards, readable in time. He who opens the scroll (Rev 5) is he who can calculate the temporal Name — and the eternal tier, the anchors, were never sealed.
