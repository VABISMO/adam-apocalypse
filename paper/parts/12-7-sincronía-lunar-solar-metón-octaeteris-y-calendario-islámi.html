7. Lunar–solar synchrony: Meton, octaeteris, and the Islamic calendar

Constants (days): synodic month = 29.530589; draconic month = 27.212221; anomalistic month = 27.554550; tropical year = 365.24219; eclipse year = 346.620083.

The tropical year contains 365.24219 / 29.530589 = 12.36827 synodic months. No integer number of lunations closes the solar year; hence all lunisolar calendars require intercalation.

The Hebrew (metonic) calendar inserts 7 intercalary months in 19 years: 12 common years (12 months) + 7 leap years (13 months) = 12×12 + 7×13 = 144 + 91 = 235 lunations = 19 solar years. (See §14 on 144.)

The Islamic (hijri) calendar is pure lunar, without intercalation (Qur'an 9:36–37 forbids nasīʾ): 12 synodics = 354.367 d; it drifts 10.875 d/year relative to the solar.

  • 33 Islamic years: 33 × 10.875 = 358.9 d ≈ 1 solar year (365 d) → the hijri year returns to the same season every ~33 years.
  • Verification: 33×354.367 = 11 694.1 d ; 32×365.2422 = 11 687.8 d ; Δ = 6.4 d.
