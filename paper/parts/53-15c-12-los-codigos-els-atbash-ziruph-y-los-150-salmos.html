15c.12 The codes: ELS, Atbash, Ziruph — and the 150 Psalms

The codes — ELS, Atbash, Ziruph. Three traditional Hebrew letter-techniques are implemented alongside the stellar reading. Equidistant Letter Sequences (ELS) select letters at a fixed skip through the consonantal text (Witztum–Rips / Drosnin); the app (CodesTab.jsx, a JavaScript port of torahcodespython and the sophia toolkit) renders any ELS of Genesis for a given term and skip. Atbash and Temurah are the reversed-alphabet / permutation substitutions of the letters; Ziruph (Kircher’s Oedipus Aegyptiacus) pairs letters by a combinatorial table. These are reproduced as attested techniques of the rabbinic-magical tradition; the article does not adopt the disputed “Bible codes” statistical thesis (the ELS apparatus is a tool, not a claim), and the stellar reading above does not depend on it.

The 150 Psalms — one per day, matched in gematria. The 150 Psalms (Sefaria Masoretic Text, psalms_he.json) are appointed one per day by a gematria match: each Psalm’s gematria sum is matched to an equidistant-letter sequence of Genesis whose own gematria sum is a subharmonic divisor of it, so each Psalm is “read” from Genesis at the matching skip (PsalmsTab.jsx). The point is structural, not exegetical: the same alphabet that the sky is read with is the alphabet Genesis and the Psalms are written in, and the gematria match ties the two voices — Genesis (the creation text) and the Psalms (the liturgical response) — into one numeric fabric. Two voices, one gematria.
