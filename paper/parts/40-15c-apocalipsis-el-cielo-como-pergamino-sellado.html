15c. Revelation: the sky as a sealed scroll

Revelation (c. 95 CE) emerges from the same milieu as the textualized Sefer Yetzirah (merkabah + creator-letters + 360/7/12 calendar). What follows maps the numbers of Revelation onto the 3/7/12 arithmetic of the SY: the numbers are in the text (verifiable); the mapping is interpretive.
