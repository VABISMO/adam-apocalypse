15c.8 Sefer Raziel HaMalakh — “calculate to see the generations” (verified, mock of 2026-08-08)

An older sibling text frames exactly what this study operationalises: compute the planets and the zodiacal signs in their fixed order, and so read the generations from beginning to end. From the Spanish Sefer Raziel HaMalakh (p. 144 of the source PDF), a passage that reads like a specification of the Lector Caeli:

“Combina los signos y la rueda. Graba y calcula y asigna y numera. Considera los cálculos de los planetas y los signos del zodíaco. Calcula los períodos y dando vueltas los planetas. De aquellos suspendidos y los signos del zodíaco en el orden perpetuo, ve con la luz. Calcula en orden para ver las generaciones. Prepara para verlos desde el principio hasta el final.

Está escrito, quien actúa y crea, proclama las generaciones desde el principio, antes de la creación del universo. Para comprender las acciones de cada hombre en la rectitud y la maldad, decreta sobre cada uno. De acuerdo a las obras, prepara las obras entre el bien y el mal.”

English translation: “Combine the signs and the wheel. Engrave and compute and assign and number. Consider the reckonings of the planets and the signs of the zodiac. Compute the periods and the turning planets. Of those suspended and the signs of the zodiac in their perpetual order, see with the light. Compute in order to see the generations. Prepare to see them from the beginning to the end. It is written: he who acts and creates proclaims the generations from the beginning, before the creation of the universe. To understand the actions of each man in righteousness and in wickedness, he decrees upon each one. According to the works, he prepares the works between good and evil.”

The “144” the eye reads as ve144 con la luz is the page number the text extractor merged into “ve con la luz” — a real textual fact of the document (the passage sits on p. 144). 144 = 12² = the New Jerusalem wall (Rev 21:17) / the 144,000 sealed (Rev 7:4) of §15c.4. The coincidence of the page number with 12² is just that — a coincidence of pagination, not an encoded signal; the text itself says “see, with the light”, not “144”.

Verdict. The Raziel instruction is not metaphor. Calculate the planets = the astronomy-engine ecliptic longitudes; the signs in perpetual order = the 12 simples = the 12 fixed 30° sectors; see the generations from beginning to end = the Reader (the readable names of any one day) + the Predictor (the legible days across a whole year).
