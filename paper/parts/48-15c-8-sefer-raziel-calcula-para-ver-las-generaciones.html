15c.8 Sefer Raziel HaMalakh — “calculate to see the generations” (verified)

An older sibling text frames exactly what this study operationalises: compute the planets and the zodiacal signs in their fixed order, and so read the generations from beginning to end. From the Sefer Raziel HaMalakh (p. 144 of the source PDF), a passage that reads like a specification of the Lector Caeli:

“Combine the signs and the wheel. Engrave and compute and assign and number. Consider the reckonings of the planets and the signs of the zodiac. Compute the periods and the turning planets. Of those suspended and the signs of the zodiac in their perpetual order, see with the light. Compute in order to see the generations. Prepare to see them from the beginning to the end.

It is written: he who acts and creates proclaims the generations from the beginning, before the creation of the universe. To understand the actions of each man in righteousness and in wickedness, he decrees upon each one. According to the works, he prepares the works between good and evil.”

The “144” the eye reads as see [144] with the light is the page number the text extractor merged into “see with the light” — a real textual fact of the document (the passage sits on p. 144). 144 = 12² = the New Jerusalem wall (Rev 21:17) / the 144,000 sealed (Rev 7:4) of §15c.4. The coincidence of the page number with 12² is just that — a coincidence of pagination, not an encoded signal; the text itself says “see, with the light”, not “144”.

The planets Raziel and the Sefer Yetzirah knew are the seven classical bodies the SY assigns to the seven doubles — Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn. The three modern planets (Uranus, Neptune, Pluto) are not in the SY and the apparatus excludes them entirely: every reading, every occupied letter, every readable name below is computed from those seven alone.

Verdict. The Raziel instruction is not metaphor. Calculate the planets = the astronomy-engine ecliptic longitudes of the seven classical bodies; the signs in perpetual order = the 12 simples = the 12 fixed 30° sectors; see the generations from beginning to end = the Reader (the readable names of any one day) + the curated scan (the readable conjunctions across ten millennia, from the ancestors to those to come).
