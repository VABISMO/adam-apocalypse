Open hypotheses:

  • Variable precession: 50.29″/year is constant; real precession accelerates/decelerates (IAU 2006 model with harmonic terms). Era dates carry ~tens of years of uncertainty from this term, not propagated. Not closeable by elementary astronomical calculation.
  • Basmala and the 19-code (not astronomical): the arithmetic facts (19 letters, 114 = 6×19) are reported; Khalifa's "19-code hypothesis" is not evaluated (a disputed statistical claim, not an astronomical fact).
  • Tetramorph: the faces↔fixed signs correspondence is traditional exegesis (Ezekiel/Revelation); it is not "demonstrated" astronomically, only shown that they are at 90°.
  • Decanatal attribution of the 72 angels (§15b.4): the mechanical 72×3 extraction is demonstrated; the angel↔decanate (5°) assignment is a structural hypothesis, not an empirical one.
  • Specific astronomical attribution of Toric gematria — hypothesis (§6.3).

The distinction between (a) intention of the practice [demonstrated] and (b) specific astronomical attribution of Toric gematria [hypothesis] is the epistemic core of the work. The stellar-letter reading was, historically, a divinatory technique — attested across cultures (Persian, Hebrew) and documented in the Sefer Yetzirah, Sefer Raziel, and Gaffarel (1629/1650); the apparatus replicates it from those instructions.
