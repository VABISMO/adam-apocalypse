9.5 Masoretic textual preservation

The 37×73 = 2701 structure of Genesis 1:1 gains relevance in the context of Masoretic textual preservation (6th–10th c. CE): the Masoretes developed a counting system to prevent alterations to the Torah —exact number of letters, words, and verses of each book; central letter and central word marked (the central word of the Torah is דָּרֹשׁ דָּרַשׁ, darosh darash, Leviticus 10:16)—. In that framework, so loaded a factorization of the first verse (2701 = 37×73, 73rd triangular, 2701+1072 = 3773) functions as a verifiable seal: any change of letters would alter the sum and, with it, the factorization. Note, however, that the test of §13 shows that the mirror-palindrome 2701→3773 is not selective (~38 % of any verse fulfills it), so the operative seal is the specific 37×73 factorization of 2701, not the generalized mirror.
