9.6 Cross-cultural corroboration (what coincides, independent traditions)

The constants of the system are not idiosyncratic to the Sefer Yetzirah: they appear, verifiably and independently, in other traditions. That they coincide does not prove the system; it corroborates it without positing borrowing. Reproducible calculation in calc_crosscultural.mjs.

Cross-cultural synthesis. The "73 = solar year" (§9.4) —the real celestial relation of the factors of Genesis 1:1— is independently encoded in the Maya calendar once (Calendar Round 73 Tzolkin = 52 Haab = 18 980 d; the Haab shares the 365-day civil year but is organized as 18×20+5, not 73 pentads); Meton (19/235) was rediscovered independently by China. Decimal-positional gematria, by contrast, is shared by Greek (isopsephy), Hebrew, and Arabic (abjad) by shared descent (all derive from the Greek/Semitic alphabetic numeral system, 6th–2nd c. BCE), not by independent invention; it therefore does not count as independent corroboration. The strongest Islamic parallel of the letter↔star correspondence is Ibn Arabi (d.1240, Futūḥāt ch.198): 28 letters ↔ 28 lunar mansions ↔ 7 planets ↔ days, but it is lunar (28), not solar (12+7+3), and Ibn Arabi himself calls the mansions a "convention". The strongest independent corroborations are thus the Maya (73×5, Calendar Round) and the Chinese (Meton).
