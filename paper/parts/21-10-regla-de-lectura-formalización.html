10. Reading rule (formalization)

Let O(t) ⊆ {ה,ו,ז,ח,ט,י,ל,נ,ס,ע,צ,ק} be the set of simple letters whose sign is occupied by at least one of the 7 classical bodies (Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn — the Sefer Yetzirah's 7 doubles) at instant t. Let S(w) be the set of simple letters of word w (after normalizing finals to regulars). Uranus, Neptune, and Pluto are not in the SY, carry no Hebrew letter, and are excluded from O(t) entirely; every reading uses the 7 doubles the SY names.

Definition (legibility). w is legible at t ⟺ S(w) ⊆ O(t).

Reuse rule. Mothers and doubles are always available and reusable. A simple is read if its sign is occupied; repeating a simple does not require conjunction (membership, not consumption). Words without simples (ברא "created", אב "father", שבת "sabbath", אמת "truth") are always legible.

Validation (tests.mjs, 88 green assertions): 12 occupied → 6045 legible words; only Virgo → 634; 0 signs → 377 always legible.

The discard is measurable and massive. For Genesis 1:1, over 2024–2030 (2557 days — two leap years, 2024 and 2028), only 2 days (0.078 %) are legible with the 7 classical bodies → the positional rule discards 99.9 % of dates. For יהוה, legible most of the year (~monthly cadence). The discard is not arithmetic (palindrome, §13.2) but positional: what distinguishes a valid reading from an invalid one is which signs are occupied, not the sum of its letters.
