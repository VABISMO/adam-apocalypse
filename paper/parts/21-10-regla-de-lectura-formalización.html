10. Reading rule (formalization)

Let O(t) ⊆ {ה,ו,ז,ח,ט,י,ל,נ,ס,ע,צ,ק} be the set of simple letters whose sign is occupied by at least one of the 10 bodies (Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune, Pluto) at instant t. Let S(w) be the set of simple letters of word w (after normalizing finals to regulars).

Two scopes, one rule. The Sefer Yetzirah assigns its 7 doubles to the 7 classical bodies (Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn) — Uranus, Neptune, and Pluto are not in the SY and are not part of its letter assignment. The live Reader of the Sky app therefore enumerates the readable names of any date over the 7 classical bodies only (the SY reading set). The present formalization keeps all 10 bodies in O(t) for one specific purpose: the secular-window analysis of §12, where the two slowest planets — Neptune (Aries) and Pluto (Aquarius) — are precisely what anchor the ~491-year Genesis 1:1 legibility windows. The two scopes are distinct and both are faithful to the design: the app's daily reading uses the 7 doubles the SY names; the paper's §12 cadence uses the slow modern pair the SY could not have named, to mark the era-marker verse. Dropping Neptune–Pluto from O(t) would dissolve the §12 windows; keeping them there does not add letters to the SY's 7 — it only supplies the secular anchor the window argument requires.

Definition (legibility). w is legible at t ⟺ S(w) ⊆ O(t).

Reuse rule. Mothers and doubles are always available and reusable. A simple is read if its sign is occupied; repeating a simple does not require conjunction (membership, not consumption). Words without simples (ברא "created", אב "father", שבת "sabbath", אמת "truth") are always legible.

Validation (tests.mjs, 88 green assertions): 12 occupied → 6045 legible words; only Virgo → 634; 0 signs → 377 always legible.

The discard is measurable and massive. For Genesis 1:1, over 2024–2030 (2557 days — two leap years, 2024 and 2028), only 293 days (11.5 %) are legible → the positional rule discards 88.5 % of dates. For יהוה, legible most of the year (~monthly cadence). The discard is not arithmetic (palindrome, §13.2) but positional: what distinguishes a valid reading from an invalid one is which signs are occupied, not the sum of its letters.
