16. Limitations

  1. Tropical mapping. The simples are assigned to 30° tropical sectors (§4), not to IAU constellations. A documented design decision, not correctable without breaking the system.
  2. n = 6. The historical correlations are an observational hypothesis, not a statistical proof.
  3. Modern hermetic framework. The operationalization of the SY as a reading of the sky is not classical kabbalistic practice; it is a contemporary synthesis.
  4. Lexicon. Strong (OpenScriptures), 6045 consonantal roots + 290 curated Spanish glosses; the rest English. Incomplete for post-biblical Hebrew.
  5. Palindrome result. The Genesis 1 nucleus palindrome excess (§13.3) is very close to the 0.05 threshold (p ≈ 0.076; the ancients did not apply Markov-chain nulls, so a close match is a valid signal) and not multiple-comparisons-corrected.
