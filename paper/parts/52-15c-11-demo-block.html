Secondary Monarchy (David/Solomon) cluster — the correspondence is not exclusive to Patriarchs. On the broader |O|≥3 alignments the Monarchy names form a coherent secondary group: the peak −9758-07-08 (|O|=4) reads 94 Monarchy names (31% of its 302 biblical names) including Abigail, Achimelek, Jerachmeel, Shealtiel, Malkiram, Achishachar — the Davidic/royal-line onomasticon — alongside the dominant Patriarchs/Conquest core (155, 51%). So a single rare alignment surfaces a coherent David/Solomon-era name cluster; Patriarchs/Conquest remains the overall dominant period on every peak, but the secondary clusters show the correspondence reaches every biblical period, each as a coherent group on the alignments whose occupied letters select it.

Non-recurrence (stellar-driven, anti-random). The long (len≥5) biblical names co-occur at avg 8.80 per peak (100% of peaks with ≥3 together); 32 of the 50 longest readable names appear on a single one of the 10 dated conjunctions only. Their recurrence gaps run to 5181 y (Baal-Tamar), with 3 gaps ≥2000 y, 16 ≥1000 y, 86 ≥500 y. A specific letter-cluster |O| reassembles only when the planets return to the same sign-occupancy — astronomically millennial — so the group appears once and vanishes; a uniform random draw would keep a regular baseline frequency. The clustered millennial-gap bursts are the anti-random, stellar-driven signature.
