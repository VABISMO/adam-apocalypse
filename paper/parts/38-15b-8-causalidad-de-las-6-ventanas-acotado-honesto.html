15b.8 Causality of the 6 windows

Two tests (calc_windows_causality.mjs): (A) Cadence — the 6 events (Axial −427, Temple 61, Justinian 552, Schism 1043, Reformation 1535, Present 2025) are spaced 488, 491, 491, 492, 490 years (mean 490.4), identical to the cadence of the astronomical windows and to the Neptune–Pluto synodic (~492.3 y): they share cadence. (B) Regularity — statistic R = Σ|spacᵢ − mean| = 5.6 (perfect grid → R→0); under a null of 6 uniform points in the same range (2452 y), P(R ≤ 5.6) < 5×10⁻⁶ (0/200 000): the regularity is very high against chance. The shared 491-year cadence is real and very regular.
