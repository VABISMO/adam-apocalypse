Frequently used short prayers:

(Also available in Avestan and Transcription fonts)

  • Kem Na Mazda (exorcism)
  • Padyab-Kusti (ablution and formula for tying the kusti)
  • Baj before meals
  • Baj when answering the call of nature
  • Nirang-i Abezar
  • The Gah dedications
  • Srosh Baj

(Also available in Avestan and Transcription fonts)
