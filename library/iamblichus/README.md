# library/iamblichus — De Mysteriis (On the Mysteries of the Egyptians, Chaldeans, and Assyrians)

This folder holds **Iamblichus' *De Mysteriis*** (Περὶ τῶν Αἰγυπτιακῶν Μυστηρίων), the
foundational late-antique treatise on theurgy, the divine, and the ritual philosophy of
the Egyptians / Chaldeans / Assyrians. Translated by **Thomas Taylor** — the Neoplatonist
translator whose work is central to the hermetic / theurgic lineage this project traces.

## Files

- `iamblichus_de_mysteriis_taylor_1821.txt` — the **complete** Taylor 1821 translation,
  OCR plain-text (551 KB). Taylor arranges the work into **10 Sections (I–X)**, each
  divided into numbered chapters — the traditional 10 "books" of *De Mysteriis*.
  Verified structure: Section I begins at line 1366; Section X at line 12619; the text
  closes with **"THE END."** (line 14463) followed by Taylor's catalog of other works.

## Completeness note

Spans all 10 sections of *De Mysteriis* with Taylor's introductory matter and additional
notes. OCR plain-text from a 200-year-old printing: occasional character-recognition
artefacts (Greek breathings, old ligatures) are present but the text is complete and
searchable. For quotation, cross-check against a modern critical edition (Clarke/Dillon/
Hershbell, 2003 — under copyright, not reproduced here).
