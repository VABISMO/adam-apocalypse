# library/zohar — Zohar (selected portions), trans. S. L. MacGregor Mathers (1887)

> **IMPORTANT — this is SELECTIONS, not the complete Zohar.** Mathers' *The Kabbalah
> Unveiled* translates only **three** of the books of the Zohar: (1) the *Book of
> Concealed Mystery* (Idra Rabba Qadisha / Greater Holy Assembly material), (2) the
> *Greater Holy Assembly* (Idra Rabba), and (3) the *Lesser Holy Assembly* (Idra Zuta),
> translated from Knorr von Rosenroth's Latin *Kabbala Denudata* and collated with the
> Chaldee/Hebrew. The full Zohar runs to thousands of pages and the complete modern
> English translations (Soncino 1931–34, Pritzker/Stanford 2004–) are **copyrighted** and
> are NOT reproduced here. This folder hosts the public-domain Mathers selections only.

- All three books present: Book of Concealed Mystery (Chapters I–II+), Greater Holy

## Why this edition

The complete Zohar's modern English translations (Soncino, Pritzker) are copyrighted and
excluded by the project IRON RULE. The only public-domain English Zohar material is
Mathers' 1887 selections, hosted here with a clear "selections, not complete" flag.
