# Unheard-of Curiosities — James Gaffarel (1650, Chilmead English)

`gaffarel_unheard_curiosities_1650_eebo.txt` — the complete clean transcription
(CC0, EEBO-TCP / Text Creation Partnership) of the 1650 Chilmead English edition
of Gaffarel's *Curiositez inouyes*: the talismanical sculpture of the Persians, the
horoscope of the patriarchs, and the reading of the stars. (Non-Latin — Hebrew/
Arabic — passages are marked `〈 in non-Latin alphabet 〉`.)

Cited by the Revelations → Persian/Avestan tab and the paper: stars ranged in the
heavens in the form of Hebrew letters; the heavens are a book (Isa 34:4); Part IV,
ch. XIII on the celestiall writing in Hebrew characters.

`gaffarel_1650_part4_reading_of_stars.txt` and `gaffarel_1650_structure.txt` are
curated extracts of the specifically-cited section and the table of contents.
