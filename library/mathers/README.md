# The Key of Solomon the King (Clavicula Salomonis) — S.L. MacGregor Mathers (1888)

`mathers_key_of_solomon_the_king_1888.txt` — the complete text of S. Liddell
MacGregor Mathers' *The Key of Solomon the King (Clavicula Salomonis)*, now first
translated and edited from ancient manuscripts in the British Museum.
George Redway, London, 1888.

## Edition & contents (verified complete)
- Preface, Preliminary Discourse, Introduction (editor's notes on the MSS.)
- **Book I** — Chapters I–XVIII, including the full chapter on the Holy Pentacles
  (the planetary talismans; 139 "Pentacle" references).
- **Book II** — Chapters I–XXII (the master's conduct, instruments, circle,
  suffumigations, sacrifice, characters, and consecration of the book).
- Appendix: **The Mystical Alphabets** (the secret writing alphabets — Malachim,
  passing of the river, etc.).
- Ends with the printer's imprint and "THE END".

## Relevance to the project
The Clavicula is the central grimoire of Solomonic planetary magic: the
construction and consecration of the planetary pentacles, the magical alphabets
mapped to Hebrew letters, and the angelic/divine-name invocations (Tetragrammaton,
Shaddai, Elohim, etc.) — the operational side of the letter–planet–sign system the
paper documents at the cosmological level.
