# Aleister Crowley — primary works

This folder hosts two complete public-domain works by Aleister Crowley
(b. 1875 – d. 1 December 1947).

## (a) Liber AL vel Legis (The Book of the Law), 1904 — PUBLIC DOMAIN

`crowley_liber_al_vel_legis_1904.txt` — the complete *Liber AL vel Legis sub
figura CCXX*, "as delivered by XCIII = 418 to DCLXVI." Includes the
Introduction and all three chapters:
- **Chapter I** (Nuit) — 66 verses.
- **Chapter II** (Hadit) — 75 verses.
- **Chapter III** (Ra-Hoor-Khuit) — 90 verses.
Ends with the correct closing: "There is no law beyond Do what thou wilt.
Love is the law, love under will. The priest of the princes, Ankh-f-n-khonsu."

### Public-domain basis
Dictated Cairo, 8–10 April 1904; privately printed 1904. In the United States it
is a pre-1928 publication and therefore in the public domain. In life+70
jurisdictions Crowley died in 1947, so it entered the public domain on
1 January 2018. The source scan is the original 1904 printing on archive.org.
(No copyrighted modern critical edition is used.)

### Source
https://archive.org/details/CrowleyTheBookOfTheLaw
("Crowley, Aleister - 1904 - The Book of the Law", DjVu text derivative).

## (b) Magick in Theory and Practice, 1929 — PUBLIC DOMAIN

`crowley_magick_in_theory_and_practice_1929.txt` — the complete *Magick in
Theory and Practice* (Book 4, Part III), by "The Master Therion" (Aleister
Crowley). Verified complete:
- Chapter 0 through **Chapter XXI** (21 chapters).
- **Appendix I** through **Appendix VII** (7 appendices).
- Ends at the close of the syllabus of magick implements (the Lamp): "…'I am
  armed, I am armed.' {436}" — the proper end of the work.

### Public-domain basis
First published 1929 (Paris: Lecram Press, privately printed). Copyright term
analysis:
- **life+70 jurisdictions** (UK, EU, etc.): Crowley died 1 Dec 1947 → public
  domain from 1 January 2018.
- **United States**: a 1929 publication carries a 95-year term from publication
  date → public domain from 1 January 2025. As of the current date (2026) it is
  therefore public domain in the U.S. as well.

No copyrighted modern critical edition is used; the source is a scan of a
reprint of the 1929 text (the Castle Books, New York, reprint, which reproduces
Crowley's 1929 text). Editorial insertions from the source scan (a "Based on
the Castle Books edition" note and an unrelated "Loved this book?" promotional
tail) have been stripped so the file contains only Crowley's text.

### Source
https://archive.org/details/MagickInTheoryAndPracticeAleisterCrowley
(DjVu text derivative of the reprint of the 1929 text.)

## Completeness evidence (both)
- Liber AL: all three chapters present; correct final lines (Ankh-f-n-khonsu).
- Magick: Chapter 0–XXI + Appendices I–VII all present; ends at the Lamp
  syllabus {436}.

## Relevance to the project
*Magick in Theory and Practice* is Crowley's systematic technical exposition of
ritual magic — the apparatus of pentacles, talismans, divine names, and the
correspondences of planets, signs, and Hebrew letters — operating squarely
within the Solomonic/Hermetic letter–planet–sign framework the paper documents.
*Liber AL vel Legis* is the foundational Thelemic text whose cosmology (Nuit =
the starry night-sky / space of all possibilities; Hadit = the point / the
manifestor) engages the stellar-letter imagination the project studies.
