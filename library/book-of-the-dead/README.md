# The Book of the Dead — Papyrus of Ani (Budge)

`book_of_the_dead_papyrus_of_ani_budge.txt` — the complete English translation of
the Papyrus of Ani by E. A. Wallis Budge: the hymns, the Negative Confession, all
chapters/spells and rubrics. Source: the clean e-text (ed. Ali B. Ali-Dinar,
University of Pennsylvania African Studies Center), originally from Arthur R. McGee.

Sothic 1461-year Sirius cycle, 72 conspirators of Set).
