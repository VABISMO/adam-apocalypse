# library/paracelsus — The Hermetic and Alchemical Writings of Paracelsus (A.E. Waite, ed., 1894, 2 vols)

This folder holds the **complete two-volume** English edition of Paracelsus' hermetic and
alchemical writings, edited with a biographical preface, elucidatory notes, a Hermetic
vocabulary, and index by **Arthur Edward Waite** — the standard 1894 collection of the
Swiss physician-alchemist-hermeticist Aureolus Philippus Theophrastus Bombast von
Hohenheim ("Paracelsus the Great", 1493–1541).

## Completeness note

Both volumes are present and complete — Vol I covers Hermetic Chemistry (the metallic
transmutation / spagyric tractates), Vol II covers Hermetic Medicine and Hermetic
Philosophy (the Archidoxies, the philosophy of the microcosm, etc.), with Waite's
biographical preface, Hermetic vocabulary, notes, and index. OCR plain-text from the
1894 printing; minor recognition artefacts, but complete and searchable.
