## Files

- `ars_magna_generalis_ultima_1517.txt` — complete DjVu OCR text derivative of the 1517
  Latin print, **41,665 lines / ~1.17 MB**. OCR of an early-16th-century gothic typeface,
  so the opening pages and decorative pages are noisy (scattered glyph artifacts), but the
  body Latin is legible: the introductorium opens "Incipit introductoꝛiũ magne artis
  generalis reuerendiſſimi doctoꝛis illuminati Magiſtri Raymundi Zullij…" and the text runs
  through all distinctiones to the colophon at the end. This is the Ars **Generalis
  Ultima** (not the Ars Brevis; the Ars Brevis only appears as the appended *imago*
  summary near the close).

## Completeness evidence

- Title page + introductorium present at head.
- Section/capitulum markers run continuously through Distinctio XII and chapter numbers
  into the 90s (e.g. "Undͤecima pars", "Duodecima pars De habituatiõe", "ad capitulum 93").
- Colophon at the close: "…ars magna:generalis et vltima…cum cottationibus marginalibus et
  tabula siue indice" — the standard close of the edition.
- This is the complete 1517 print of the Ars Generalis Ultima.

## Note on edition choice

The brief asked for the *Ars Generalis Ultima* (1305). This 1517 print is the complete
Latin Ars Generalis Ultima. It is *not* the shorter *Ars Brevis*; the Ars Brevis is
appended only as the summary *imago artis generalis* near the end, as was conventional.
