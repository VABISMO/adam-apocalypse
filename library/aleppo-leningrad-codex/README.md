## What this is

A scan of the *facsimile edition* of the Aleppo Codex — i.e. photographs of the
actual 10th-century manuscript leaves. This is the **manuscript image scan**
entry (the machine-readable Masoretic *text* is the separate `hebrew-bible`
entry, derived from the Leningrad Codex).

## Completeness — and the missing Torah

The Aleppo Codex is **partly lost**: the **Torah (Pentateuch) and parts of some
other books were destroyed** in the 1947 Aleppo synagogue fire. What survives
— essentially Nevi'im, most of Ketuvim, and the Masoretic notes — is what is
reproduced here. Per the archive.org item description, this *facsimile
edition* scan is in fact **the most complete extant reproduction**: it
includes all extant pages reproduced in the facsimile, and each page is
numbered with its biblical book/chapter/verse indicated at the bottom. (A
separate "direct scan from the manuscript" item, `Aleppo_Codex`, is missing
parts of some books that this facsimile edition retains.) So this is the most
complete available form of the surviving Aleppo Codex.
