# library/eliphas-levi — Transcendental Magic: its Doctrine and Ritual (Éliphas Lévi, tr. A.E. Waite, 1896)

This folder holds **Éliphas Lévi's *Dogme et Rituel de la Haute Magie*** in the complete
English translation by **Arthur Edward Waite** — the 19th-century occult classic that
synthesised the Tarot, the Kabbalah, the Tetragrammaton / Pentagram symbolism, and the
hermetic-magical tradition into the framework central to this project's magi index and
stellar-letter lineage.

  also exist; the 1896 uoft scan is the first edition and the cleanest.)

## Files

- `levi_transcendental_magic_waite_1896.txt` — the **complete** Waite 1896 translation
  (957 KB, ~18,600 lines), spanning **both** parts:
  - **Part I — The Doctrine (Dogme)**: Introduction + Chapters I–XXII (The Candidate;
    Pillars of the Temple; Triangle of Solomon; The Tetragram; The Pentagram; Magical
    Equilibrium; The Fiery Sword; Realisation; Initiation; The Kabbalah; The Magic Chain;
    The Great Work; Necromancy; Transmutations; Black Magic; Bewitchments; Astrology;
    Charms and Philtres; The Stone of the Philosophers — Elagabalus; The Universal
    Medicine; Divination; Summary and General Key of the Four Secret Sciences).
  - **Part II — The Ritual (Rituel)**: Introduction (p. 175) + Chapters I–XXII
    (Preparations; Magical Equilibrium; Triangle of Pantacles; Conjuration of the Four;
    The Blazing Pentagram; The Medium and Mediator; The Septenary of Talismans; A Warning
    to the Impudent; The Ceremonial of Initiates; The Key of Occultism; The Triple Chain;
    The Great Work; Necromancy; Transmutations; The Sabbath of the Sorcerers; Witchcraft
    and Spells; The Writing of the Stars; Philtres and Magnetism; The Mastery of the Sun;
    The Thaumaturge; The Science of the Prophets; The Book of Hermes) + the Supplement to
    the Ritual.
  - Closes with **"FINIS."** (line 17584), followed only by the library pocket card.

## Completeness note

Full Waite translation of both *Dogme* (Part I) and *Rituel* (Part II), with Waite's
biographical preface on Lévi. OCR plain-text from the 1896 printing; minor recognition
artefacts (e.g. "Eitual" for "Ritual" in some headers), but complete and searchable.
